## Building the Future of Home Services

nbound is a technology company developing an intelligent, unified operating platform for the home-service industry.

We're building software that connects every part of a service business, from customer acquisition and scheduling to field operations, financial management, and business intelligence.

Today, home-service companies often rely on disconnected applications to manage their operations. This creates fragmented information, repetitive administrative work, and unnecessary complexity.

We believe businesses deserve something better.

**Our mission is to make running a service business simpler, more intelligent, and more connected.**

## The Platform

nbound brings the essential capabilities of business management together in one system.

| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| Customer Management | Customer relationships, properties, service histories, and communications |
| Scheduling & Dispatch | Appointment management, workforce scheduling, and intelligent dispatch |
| Field Operations | Job management, technician workflows, assets, and service documentation |
| Financial Management | Estimates, invoicing, payments, and memberships |
| Inventory & Services | Inventory management, service catalogs, and intelligent pricing |
| Business Intelligence | Operational reporting, financial analytics, and performance insights |
| Growth | Lead management, customer engagement, and business growth tools |
| Collaboration | Connected workspaces, project information, and team coordination |

Our platform is designed for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other home-service businesses, from independent operators to multi-location organizations.

## Perseus — The Intelligence Layer

**Intelligence should be built into the system, not added as an afterthought.**

Perseus is the AI intelligence layer powering nbound.

We're developing Perseus to understand operational context, assist with business decisions, and help execute workflows across the platform.

Its capabilities and development focus include:

- Conversational AI for customer interactions and communication.
- Intelligent appointment scheduling and coordination.
- Autonomous dispatch and workforce assignment.
- Context-aware operational assistance.
- Business intelligence and actionable insights.
- Workflow automation across connected business systems.

Our objective is to move beyond AI-powered features toward software that can intelligently coordinate real business operations.

## Connected From Start to Finish

Consider a typical service interaction:

**Customer Contact → Appointment → Dispatch → Field Service → Invoice → Payment**

Each stage depends on the information created during the previous one.

With nbound, these workflows are designed to operate within a single connected environment.

The result is less manual coordination, greater operational visibility, and a more consistent experience for customers and teams.

## Our Engineering Philosophy

Building software for businesses operating in the real world demands more than an attractive interface.

We prioritize five principles:

**01. Reliability by design**

Business-critical workflows require consistent, predictable behavior and resilient infrastructure.

**02. Intelligence with accountability**

Automation should be explainable, observable, and designed around appropriate human oversight.

**03. Unified architecture**

Shared information and connected capabilities should replace unnecessary fragmentation.

**04. Security as a foundation**

Protecting business data, maintaining appropriate access controls, and designing secure systems are fundamental engineering responsibilities.

**05. Thoughtful product design**

Sophisticated software should feel intuitive. Complexity belongs in the engineering, not in the user experience.
