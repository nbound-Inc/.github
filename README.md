## About nbound

nbound is building a unified operating platform for the home-service industry, bringing the tools, workflows, and intelligence required to run a modern service business into one connected system.

From the first customer interaction to the final payment, nbound is designed to simplify operations, eliminate fragmented software, and give business owners greater visibility and control.

We're starting with HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other field-service businesses, with a broader vision of becoming the infrastructure that powers how service companies operate, grow, and scale.

Our approach is simple: **business software should work together by default, not require businesses to assemble and maintain a collection of disconnected tools.**

## What We're Building

### An integrated business operating platform

nbound connects the essential functions of a home-service business through a shared operational system.

**Customer relationships & communication**

Manage customer relationships, properties, service histories, leads, appointments, and communications from one place.

**Scheduling & intelligent dispatch**

Coordinate technicians, manage workforce availability, schedule appointments, and support intelligent job assignment through connected scheduling and dispatch workflows.

**Field operations**

Give teams access to the information and tools they need to complete work, document services, manage equipment, and stay connected across the business.

**Financial operations**

Connect estimates, invoicing, payments, memberships, and financial reporting to the underlying work being performed.

**Inventory & service management**

Manage inventory, equipment, services, pricing, and the resources required to deliver consistent, profitable service.

**Business intelligence & growth**

Turn operational information into actionable insights with unified reporting, customer intelligence, and tools designed to help businesses grow.

## Meet Perseus

### Intelligence built into the operation.

Perseus is the intelligence layer of nbound.

Rather than treating artificial intelligence as a standalone feature, we're building it into the workflows businesses already depend on.

Our vision for Perseus extends across customer communication, scheduling, dispatch, operational assistance, and business intelligence.

The objective is not simply to generate responses. It's to help businesses make decisions, coordinate work, and execute routine operations with greater intelligence and less manual overhead.

**AI should be part of how the platform operates, not another tool businesses have to manage.**

## One Connected Workflow

A customer calls. An appointment is created. The right technician is assigned. Work is completed. An invoice is generated. Payment is collected.

These activities shouldn't require separate systems, duplicated information, or constant manual coordination.

nbound is designed around connecting the entire service lifecycle, allowing information and workflows to move naturally from one stage to the next.
